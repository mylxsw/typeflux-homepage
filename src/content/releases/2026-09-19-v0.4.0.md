Typeflux 0.4.0 shortens the wait before you can speak, keeps more useful words when recognition gets difficult, and makes the path from speech to the app you're using more dependable.

## Start speaking sooner

- A new low-latency recording path starts microphone capture earlier, so less of the beginning of your sentence is at risk of being missed.
- Optional Instant Voice Input keeps the microphone ready for 15 minutes after use. Turn it on in Settings when you want to speak again with minimal startup delay.
- Short presses enter locked recording, while a new press of an input shortcut can finish an active recording.

## Get a usable transcript more reliably

- On supported Typeflux Cloud routes, cloud recognition and local SenseVoice can run together. Typeflux briefly favors the cloud result, then uses a ready local result when it is faster or the cloud fails.
- Quiet or very short recordings without a live preview receive an adjusted audio retry, improving the chance of recovering speech that might otherwise be missed.
- Cloud routing is prepared ahead of time to reduce avoidable waiting when recording starts.

## Make shortcuts and personas your own

- Add an auxiliary dictation shortcut with its own persona—for example, use one shortcut for everyday writing and another for translation.
- The redesigned persona library separates built-in and custom personas, adds search, and makes the current default clear while you edit a different persona.
- AI rewrite wait time can be adjusted in Settings. Longer input automatically gets more time, and the original transcript remains available when rewriting cannot finish.

## Deliver text with more confidence

- Typeflux checks the active input again when the result is ready, including after you switch apps or windows.
- Rewriting selected text and inserting into apps with limited accessibility support are more reliable. When a paste cannot be confirmed, the result stays in History for recovery instead of triggering a misleading failure popup.
- Known insertion failures still offer the full result for copying.

## See what happened

- History now shows clearer recording, recognition, and AI processing details, including which recognition path was used and where time was spent.
- The Account page makes cloud credits, subscription status, renewal information, and billing recovery easier to find.
- Recording hints, processing progress, and shortcut guidance have been refined across the app.
