Typeflux Classic focuses on voice input. It includes local and cloud speech recognition, text polishing, personas, vocabulary, and voice input history. Typeflux adds Ask, AI Agent, Launcher, Ring, screenshots, screen recording, and clipboard history. Both editions use the same account, membership, and credits.

## What carries over

Your voice input history, personas, vocabulary, shortcuts, and shared settings carry over. Your sign-in, membership, and macOS permissions also carry over on the same Mac. Classic can use supported polishing models selected in Typeflux. If you use a custom model endpoint, choose a supported model in Classic.

Classic displays only the shared data. Ask conversations and other data from Typeflux-only features stay saved and are available when you reinstall Typeflux. Changes to shared settings in Classic, such as edits to personas, are available when you switch back.

## Switch from Typeflux to Classic

In Typeflux 1.0.1 or later, open **Settings → General**. Under **Updates and Data**, find the **Typeflux Classic** row and click **Switch to Typeflux Classic**. You can also use the same button in the **About Typeflux** window. Click **Switch** to confirm. These buttons appear when Classic is available.

If you used Typeflux before upgrading, the **Meet Typeflux Classic** window appears once when Classic is available. You can switch from there too.

You can switch manually by downloading **Typeflux Classic** for your Mac from the [downloads page](/releases). Quit Typeflux, open the downloaded disk image, and drag **Typeflux.app** to **Applications**. Choose **Replace** when macOS asks, then open **Typeflux Classic**.

## Switch from Classic to Typeflux

Download **Typeflux** for your Mac from the [downloads page](/releases). Quit Classic, open the downloaded disk image, and drag **Typeflux.app** to **Applications**. Choose **Replace** when macOS asks, then open **Typeflux**. Your existing data will be available, including Ask conversations saved before the switch.

Both editions use the same app file. Keep one installed at a time and leave your app data in place. Automatic updates stay on the edition you installed.
